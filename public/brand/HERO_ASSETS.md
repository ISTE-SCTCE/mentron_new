# Common landing hero assets

Updated 18 October 2026 for the requested Artesa reference treatment and release preparation.

The released page uses locally created `street-grain.svg` and `street-treads.svg`. It no longer depends on the third-party reference textures below. Original paint, graffiti, sticker, spray-can artwork and scroll treatment remain. Historical reference textures were moved out of public assets and are excluded from the delivery package.

- `hero-concrete.jpg`: background texture from https://uploads-ssl.webflow.com/64c91e4463c6984655395376/64dccfdbd12c1cc49230eeda_artesa-bg.jpg, referenced by https://www.artesa.group/.
- `hero-tire-tracks.webp`: texture from https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/64c91e4463c6984655395376/64cce24f0688e25b4850b955_img-tire-tracks.webp, referenced by https://www.artesa.group/.
- These reference textures remain third-party assets; no ownership or redistribution license is asserted here.
- `hero-paint.svg`, the tribe lettering paths, and the paper sticker are locally created artwork.
- The Mentron+ spray cans in `CommonJourney.jsx` are original SVG artwork, with local gradients, lettering, and spray mist. Their scroll motion and paint trails are implemented locally; they do not reuse the reference can image or animation code.
- Anton is distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/anton. Font and license are in `public/fonts/Anton-Regular.ttf` and `public/fonts/Anton-OFL.txt`.

The common page uses original Mentron+ copy and branding, with no Artesa corporate marks.
